Identities

Keys generated in your browser, stored in IndexedDB. Share your public key so others can send you messages and verify your identity — one key does both. Keys never leave this browser.

Channels

Channels use a symmetric key derived from a shared passphrase. Anyone who knows the passphrase can post and read — it is not a secret password.

Contacts

Contacts are other people's public credentials. Share your own via the Share snk: button on your identity cards. Paste someone else's snk: URI here to add them.
